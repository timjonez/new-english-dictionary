:::entry Adorn
Adorn (ădɔ̄·ɹn), v. Forms: 4-5 aourne, 5 aorne, 5-6 adourne, 5-7 adorne, 6- adorn. [a. OFr. aörne-r, aürne-r, aourne-r, (later adourne-r, adorne-r) :—L. adornā-re to fit out, to deck out ; f. ad to + ōrnā-re to furnish, to deck.
