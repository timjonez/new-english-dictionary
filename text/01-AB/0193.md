:::entry Afterbirth
Afterbirth (a·ftəɹbēɹþ). [AFTER- 6; in sense 1 perh. directly from Norse ; cf. Icel. eptir-burðr, eftir-burðr (c 1300), OSw. efterbörd (Ihre), Dan. efterbyrd.]
