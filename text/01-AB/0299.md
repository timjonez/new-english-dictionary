:::entry Ambush
Ambush (æ·mbuʃ), v. Forms: a. 4 enbusse, inbuche, 4-5 enbusshe, 5 embuisshe, 4-6 enbusche, 5-6 embusshe, 6-7 embush, 7- ambush. Also β. 4 abusse, abusche; γ. 4 busse, 6 busche, 7 bush. [a. OFr. embusche-r, embuissier, cogn. w. Sp. embuscar, It. imboscare:—late L. *in-boscāre, f. in in + bosc-us wood, BUSH, i.e. to place in a wood, or among the bushes. For change to am- bef. 1600, see prec. Accented ambu·sh as late as 17th c.; already in 14th the toneless en- was treated like OE. prefix an-, becoming ă-, and then falling away: enbu·sh, ăbu·sh, 'bush. In 16th c. there was a by-form IMBOSQUE, a. It. imboscāre.]
