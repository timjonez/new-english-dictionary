:::entry Alloy
Alloy (ăloi·), sb. Also 6 aloye. [a. mod.Fr. aloi:— OFr. alei, retained in Norman as alai, allai, whence our earlier word ALLAY sb.¹, which this Parisian form has since 1600 gradually displaced. Through the erroneous fancy that Fr. aloi was = d loi ‘to law,’ the word, meaning originally simple ‘combination, union,’ came to be used specially of the mixing of baser metal with gold or silver in coinage, so as to bring it to the recognized standard, and hence of the standard itself.]

1875 URE Dict. Arts II. 918  The native alloy on account of its hardness is used to point metallic pens.
