:::entry Aline, align
Aline, align (ăləi·n), v.  [ad. mod. Fr. aligne-r, f. à to + ligner, ad. L. līneā-re to line, f. līnea a LINE ; prob. due to phrase à ligne, ‘into line.’ As line is the Eng. spelling of Fr. ligne and ligner, there is no good reason for retaining the unetymological g in the derivative : see ALINER.]
