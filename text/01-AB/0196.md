:::entry † Agai·n-
† Agai·n-, [the adv.], was formerly used in many verbal combinations, which are now all obsolete ; of the simple GAIN- with which it varied, GAINSAY is the only surviving representative. As again- was originally a movable adverbial particle, as in mod. G. entgegen-kommen, it always had the main stress, agai·n-mee·ting.
