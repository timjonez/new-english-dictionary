:::entry Abundance
Abundance (ăbɒ·ndăns). Forms: 4-6 abundaunce, habundaunce, -ance ; 5-6 boundance, haboundaunce, -ance ; 4-7 aboundaunce, -ance ; 4- abundance. [a. OFr. abundance, abondance, hab- :—L. abundantia, n. of state f. abundant-em, ABUNDANT. The spelling habundance frequent in Fr. and Eng. from the 14th c. was due to the word being mistakenly supposed to be a derivative of habēre to have. An aphetic boundance, 5-6, was due to initial a being taken as the indef. art. quasi a boundance.]

1687 T. BROWN Saints in an Uproar Wks. 1730 I. 82  Abundance of worthless and fabulous scoundrels.

3. Plentifulness, or plentiful supply, of the good things of life ; superfluity, affluence, wealth.

1857 Bohn's Handbk. of Prov. 305  Abundance, like want, ruins many.
† 4. a. Added to nouns : In abundance, in large quantity, or number. (Probably after the analogy of wine enough, ships enow.) Obs. b. Adverbially before adj. : Very much, a great deal. Obs.
