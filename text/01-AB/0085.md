4. Anything that completes, finishes off, or completely equips. a. Formerly including bodily equipment, accoutrement ; b. now, a faculty or quality that completes or perfects a person for society—that adds delicacy of taste and elegance of manners to accuracy of knowledge and correctness of thought. As such faculties, besides accomplishing or perfecting their possessor, are usually also accomplished or attained by study and practice, as in sense 3, the common modern use of the word combines the two senses in that of 'an ornamental attainment or acquirement,' i.e. some study accomplished which accomplishes the student.
