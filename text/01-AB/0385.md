:::entry Aphrite
Aphrite (æ·frait). Min. [f. Gr. ἀφρ-ός foam + -ITE, = foam-stone.] A variety of carbonate of lime or calcite.
1868 DANA Min. 678  Aphrite, in its harder and more sparry variety is a foliated white pearly calcite, near argentine; in its softer kinds it approaches chalk, though lighter, pearly in lustre . . and more or less scaly in structure.

:::entry || Aphtha
|| Aphtha (æ·fþă). Path. [L. aphtha (in cl. L. always in pl. aphthæ), a. :::entry Aphthonite
