:::entry Albatross
Albatross (æ·lbătrǫs). Forms: 7 algatross, 7-8 albi-, 8 albe-, 8— albatross. [Apparently a modification of ALCATRAS, applied to the Frigate-bird, but extended through inaccurate knowledge to a still larger sea-fowl, and in this sense altered to albi-, albe-, albatross (perhaps with etymological reference to albus white, the albatross being white, while the alcatras was black).  Algatross in 16th c. may be an intermediate form; albatross has not been found bef. 1769.  The word has now passed into most of the mod. langs. (Du. albatross, G. -tross, -tros, Fr. -tros, It. -tro, Pg. -troz, Sp. -trosle), but seems to have originated in Eng. (or ? Du.)]
