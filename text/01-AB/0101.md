:::entry Achieve
Achieve (ătʃī·v), v. Forms: 4-6 acheve; 4 achyve; 5 acheive, atcheve; (6 ascheve); 6-7 atchive; 6-9 atchieve; 5- achieve. [a. Fr. acheve-r, formed from phrase à chief (venir) :—late L. ad caput venire to come to a head with, to bring to a head, to finish. An aphetic form, common in ME. but now obs., was CHIEVE. Northern writers had also ESCHEVE, with the prefix erroneously refashioned by form-assoc. with words in a- for original es-, as achape for eschape, escape.]
