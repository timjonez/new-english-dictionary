:::entry Aggregate
Aggregate (æ·grĭgĕt, -ėt), ppl. a. and sb.; also 4-5 aggregat. [ad. L. aggregāt-us united in a flock, associated, pa. pple. of aggregā-re, f. ag- = ad- to + gregā-re to collect ; f. grex, greg-em a flock.]

† 7. Gram. Collective. Obs.

:::entry Aggregation
Aggregation (ægrĭgēⁱ·ʃən). [n. of action f. AGGREGATE v., as if ad. L. *aggregātiōn-em f. aggregā-re. Cf. L. congregātio, and late Fr. agrégation.]
